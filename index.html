What the Wicked Want

By Marlee Corvin

Shane Calder submits himself to pain night after night, but for the officer with wounds new and old, being forced to trust is the real torture.

A serial killer is stalking New York's fetish clubs, leaving gutted victims bound in bloody displays. Shane's undercover role as a submissive has him submersed in the scene as bait. When his solo investigation stalls, he must work with two FBI agents who seem more interested in a mistake from his past than the present case.

Shane can take the pain, but learning to be vulnerable is harder. It hurts more.

And no one can last forever.

Every body has a breaking point.
